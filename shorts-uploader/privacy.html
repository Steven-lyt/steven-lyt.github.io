Privacy Policy for Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

It asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, so that it can upload videos to the YouTube channel of the Google account that signs in. It does not read the owner's other Google data, and it does not access the data of any other user.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored in a file on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server run by the developer or to any third party. Titles and descriptions of uploaded videos are sent only to YouTube.

Sharing

Shorts Uploader does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
