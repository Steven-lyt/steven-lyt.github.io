How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter English word in six tries.

Each guess must be a real word. After each guess, the tiles change colour:

Green: right letter, right spot.

Yellow: the letter is in the word, but in another spot.

Grey: the letter is not in the word.

Daily: one new word every day, the same for everyone.

Unlimited: play as many words as you like.

Hard mode: every hint you find must be used in your next guess.
